May this archived theme/plugin restoration remain as part of that recovery workflow?

Declarations use the OzekiResilience\M1 namespace. Existing owr_ option/meta identifiers and hooks are retained for compatibility with saved state and integrations. Do you require their migration before approval, or may these existing identifiers remain?
