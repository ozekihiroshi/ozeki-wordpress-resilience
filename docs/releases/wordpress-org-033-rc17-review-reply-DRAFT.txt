Hello Plugin Review Team,

Thank you for pointing out the WP-Members installer dependency. On review, we found that recovery had replaced prepared settings with a partial projection and then regenerated defaults. We corrected the cause: recovery now preserves the destination settings established by normal setup and applies supported archived information to them. The includes/install.php include and initialization call have been removed. Missing setup is reported before replacement.

Administrators prepare trusted WordPress, themes and plugins separately. Recovery does not install or initialize third-party plugins or deploy archived software. Isolated real-S3 checks covered recovery, normal use, resaving and second recovery, with WP-Members initialization instrumented to fail if called and installed plugin files/activation state unchanged.

The proposed rc.17 also includes attachment preservation corrections. New attachment recovery points require an updated reader; existing originals and history are not rewritten.
